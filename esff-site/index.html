Where this came from

I lost a Team USA Regional by two fish.

Lower Nantahala River. The fish sat in a long, flat glide I had walked straight past. Session One, four fish. Session Two, I went straight there and landed nineteen. The Hierarchy of Stream Alignment was born that day.
